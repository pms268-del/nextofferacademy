# Worksheet: Scam Check {#wb-scam .unnumbered}

*(Chapter 5.)* Run this on any unexpected offer. **One "yes" = stop.**

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Do I have to pay anything (fees, equipment, "unlock" deposits)? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Did they contact me unsolicited via text/WhatsApp/Telegram/Signal? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Is the interview chat-only, with no named hiring manager? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Is the pay unusually high for simple work? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Is the email domain free or a look-alike? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Are they asking for my SSN/bank details before a written offer? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Did they send a check to buy equipment or forward money? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Is the job missing from the company's official careers page? | [ ] | [ ] |
| Are they pressuring me to decide today? | [ ] | [ ] |

Report: ReportFraud.ftc.gov · ic3.gov · the platform's "Report" button.
