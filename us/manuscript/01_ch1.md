## Pick your lane in 60 seconds

::: {.decision}
**Answer the first question that is true for you:**

1. **"I need money in the next 1–2 weeks."** → Run three tracks at once. (a) Generalist AI training: DataAnnotation, Outlier, TELUS Digital, Prolific. (b) Paid research studies: UserTesting, Respondent, User Interviews. (c) One freelance service built on a skill you already have (Upwork/Fiverr). Expect small, irregular amounts first. Chapter 12 has the 7-day plan.
2. **"I have deep professional expertise (law, finance, medicine, engineering, software, teaching, writing, operations)."** → Expert AI training: Mercor, Alignerr, micro1, Turing, Snorkel. Add GLG for paid expert calls. Expert listings commonly pay $40–150/hr, but they are project-based and unpredictable.
3. **"I am a master's or PhD student or recent graduate in the US."** → Handshake AI Fellowship ($50–125/hr range, official FAQ) plus Mercor and Alignerr.
4. **"I am an undergraduate or new grad with little experience."** → DataAnnotation, Outlier, Prolific, TELUS for income; Handshake jobs, YC Work at a Startup, and Wellfound for a first career role; Apple At Home Advisor (College Program) and customer-service employers for a W-2 paycheck.
5. **"I want a stable, salaried remote job."** → Targeted applications on LinkedIn, Indeed, We Work Remotely, Remotive, and Wellfound, combined with referrals (Chapter 11). Plan for a 30–90 day process: the median time to a first offer in one 2025 dataset was 68.5 days.
:::

## What "realistic" looks like

The research behind this playbook points to these planning numbers. They come from documented sources, and individual results vary widely:

- **AI-training acceptance:** typically days to about four weeks after an assessment. Rejection is often silent (DataAnnotation, Mercor, TELUS).
- **First freelance client:** documented cases range from 6 days to 6 months. "About 2 months" is a common report on the Upwork Community forum.
- **First Fiverr order:** worker reports range from about one week to several weeks, and some sellers get none.
- **Salaried remote job:** a median of 68.5 days to first offer across 461,000 tracked applications (Huntr, Q2 2025). Remote postings were 8.4% of LinkedIn jobs but drew 42% of applications.

Because no single channel is reliable, the action plan in Chapter 12 runs **income tracks and career tracks at the same time.**
