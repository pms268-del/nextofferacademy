# Other Remote Jobs: Employers, Gigs & Job Boards {#ch4}

> **In this chapter:** remote W-2 employers that hire beginners (customer service, tech support), virtual assistance, tutoring, transcription, testing and paid research, the job boards worth your time, and free public help for laid-off workers. W-2 employee roles give steadier hours, tax withholding and often benefits. Per-minute and per-task contractor roles usually do not.

## W-2 employer or contractor? Know the difference

| | W-2 employee (e.g., Amazon, Apple, Concentrix, TTEC, Welo Data's US Scout rater) | 1099 contractor (e.g., Liveops, most AI-training and gig platforms) |
|---|---|---|
| Taxes | Withheld by employer | You pay income tax plus ~15.3% self-employment tax; make quarterly estimates |
| Minimum wage & overtime | Protected | Generally not |
| Benefits | Often (after a waiting period) | No |
| Equipment | Often provided | You buy it |
| Pay | Hourly for scheduled time | Per minute, per task, or per hour actually worked |
| Upfront costs | Should be **none** | Background check or training fees sometimes required (Liveops: $25 check). Never pay to "buy a job." |

## Customer service & tech support

These are the most accessible remote W-2 jobs for beginners. Reported 2026 pay: **Concentrix ~$13–18/hr, Foundever ~$14–17/hr, TTEC ~$15–18/hr, Amazon Virtual Customer Service ~$15–19/hr, Apple At Home Advisor** (College Program listing $27.75–32.45/hr). Common requirements: a quiet, private workspace, a wired high-speed connection (many run a speed test before the offer), a background check, and **residency in a state where the employer is hiring.**

**How to apply safely:** apply only on the employer's own careers site (amazon.jobs, jobs.apple.com, jobs.concentrix.com, careers.ttec.com, foundever.com/careers). Scammers impersonate these brands through text messages, WhatsApp, and fake listings.

**Caution: contractor call-center models.** *Liveops* is a real, long-running company. Its official FAQ says agents pay a **$25 background check**, certification is unpaid, and some programs pay **25–34¢ per talk-minute**, so you are paid only while on calls, with no guaranteed earnings. *Arise* was the subject of a ProPublica investigation and a lawsuit by the D.C. Attorney General alleging that agents paid for training and equipment and were misclassified. Compute your real hourly rate before paying anything, and prefer W-2 roles.

## Virtual assistance

**BELAY** places US-based contractors with small businesses. Clients reportedly pay $42–50/hr; one anonymous report put VA pay near $20/hr; BELAY says it raised specialist pay at least 53% over four years. It expects real admin experience. Many VAs build their own client base through Upwork, Contra, and LinkedIn instead. Prompt P09 builds that profile.

## Tutoring

- **Wyzant** (Apply Now): you set your rate. Commission is reported at 25% until you earn $2,000, then 20%. You compete on profile and response speed.
- **Varsity Tutors** and **Tutor.com**: assigned sessions, lower pay (~$12–40/hr reported, with the low end most common), but no marketing needed.
- **Best practice:** start on a marketplace to get reviews, then offer private sessions to repeat students where the platform's terms allow it.

## Transcription

**Rev** (Apply Now) requires a grammar test and a transcription sample. Reported pay is about **$0.30–1.10 per audio minute**, which often works out to a low hourly rate once editing time is counted. TranscribeMe and GoTranscript are similar. AI transcription has reduced demand. Use transcription for short-term cash, not as a career.

## Testing & paid research studies

| Platform | What you do | Reported pay |
|---|---|---|
| **UserTesting** | Think-aloud website/app tests; occasional live interviews | ~$10 per 15–20 min test; $30–60 live |
| **Respondent** | Paid interviews for professionals | $50–400+ per study |
| **User Interviews** | Interviews, surveys, usability studies | Shown on each study |
| **uTest (Applause)** | Find bugs; paid per approved bug | Varies; high competition |
| **Testlio** | Hourly QA on client projects (paid trial cycle) | e.g., $18/hr listing |

These pay quickly and need no experience, but volume is unpredictable. Testers report that $10 tests disappear within minutes, so turn on notifications.

## Job boards worth your time

| Board | Best for | Cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn Jobs** (Remote filter) | All functions; recruiter outreach | Free |
| **Indeed** | Customer service, admin, sales, healthcare admin | Free |
| **We Work Remotely** | Tech, support, marketing (has "USA only" region filter) | Free |
| **Remotive** | Tech, support, AI-training listings | Free |
| **Himalayas** | Remote roles with company profiles | Free |
| **Wellfound** | Startups; salary and equity shown | Free |
| **YC Work at a Startup** | YC-backed startups | Free |
| **Handshake** | Students and recent grads | Free |
| **Remote OK, Working Nomads, Dynamite Jobs, Built In** | Supplementary listings | Free |
| **FlexJobs** (Caution) | Hand-screened listings | $2.95 trial that auto-renews (~$23.95 every 4 weeks reported) |
| **USAJOBS** (Caution) | Federal roles | Free, but the January 2025 executive order ended most federal remote work |

**Board strategy.** Remote postings attract a disproportionate share of applicants: 8.4% of LinkedIn postings drew 42% of applications. Use the "past 24 hours" filter, apply early, then contact someone on the hiring team (Chapter 11). A posting that is two weeks old has usually received hundreds of applications.

## Free public help for laid-off workers

- **Unemployment insurance:** file in the state where you worked, as soon as possible after a layoff. Use CareerOneStop's Unemployment Insurance finder to reach your state's site. Ask your state how part-time or 1099 earnings affect your weekly benefit, and report earnings exactly as required.
- **American Job Centers (~2,300 nationwide):** free career counseling, resume reviews, workshops, and access to training funds (WIOA). Many offer free premium learning platforms. Find yours at careeronestop.org.
- **Your university career office** (students and alumni): free resume reviews, mock interviews (often Big Interview or Yoodli access), and job boards. Many alumni do not know they can still use these services.
