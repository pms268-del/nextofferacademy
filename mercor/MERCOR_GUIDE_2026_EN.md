# Executive Overview & How Mercor Recruits {#m1}

> **The core message.** Getting recruited through Mercor takes more than uploading a CV and waiting. Strong candidates keep an accurate profile, use AI to discover every relevant listing, apply systematically, complete useful assessments early, show real expertise, meet the technical and regional requirements, and stay ready for the next opportunity.

**About this edition.** This English edition is written for international readers, including Indonesian applicants. Compared with the Bahasa Indonesia edition, it puts much more weight on what applicants and contractors actually report on Reddit, Glassdoor, Trustpilot, Indeed, X, independent blogs and community forums. Chapter 2, **The Community Playbook**, is the heart of the guide.

Information was checked on **4 November 2026** against Mercor's official documentation ([talent.docs.mercor.com](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/welcome)), news coverage and community sources. This is a public guide, not a personal recruitment plan. We did not inspect anyone's account or every job listing. Mercor changes its policies often, so always confirm on the official pages.

**How we label evidence:**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Explicitly stated in current official Mercor documentation |
| **Supported, limited** | Consistent with official information and reported by multiple applicants |
| **Anecdotal** | Based on one or a few individual experiences |
| **Unverified** | No adequate support, or sources contradict each other |

> **A note on Reddit.** Our research tools cannot read reddit.com directly. Reddit evidence in this guide comes from public mirror archives of r/mercor_ai and from articles that quote or analyse those threads. We link those sources. Treat every community claim as a lead to test, not a rule.

## What Mercor is

Mercor is a marketplace that connects skilled people with AI training and evaluation projects for AI labs and technology companies. Typical work includes writing and grading AI responses, building evaluation tasks, reviewing code, rating audio and video, and expert projects in law, medicine, finance, science and many languages, including Indonesian.

**You are an independent contractor, not an employee.** Mercor does not offer W-2 positions in the US and states that the work is not "employment" ([Mercor: 1099 vs W2](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/us-1099-vs-w2)). **Confirmed.** Projects are temporary. Contractors often describe projects lasting 2–8 weeks ([G2](https://g2.com/products/mercor/reviews)). **Anecdotal.**

| Type | What it looks like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Traditional job** | Employee, salary, long-term | Mercor's own corporate staff roles (not the focus of this guide) |
| **Hourly contractor project** | Paid weekly for hours tracked in Insightful | Generalist, domain expert, language evaluator |
| **Deliverable-based contract (DBC)** | Paid per output, no time tracking | Fixed-output tasks |
| **Work trial** | A **paid**, project-specific assessment with a fixed amount | Trial before a project contract |

**Generalist vs domain expert.** Generalist roles ask for writing, reasoning and attention to detail, such as writing prompts and grading AI answers. Domain-expert roles ask for provable expertise: a degree, a licence or real professional experience. They usually pay more. Community reviews say most projects expect several years of experience or advanced education, and beginners report few callbacks ([NodeMaven](https://nodemaven.com/blog/mercor-review-2026-is-mercor-ai-legit/)). **Supported, limited.**

## How candidates get matched

- **Matching system.** When your interview performance, assessments, skills and availability match a listing, your profile can be surfaced to the project team as **prequalified**. Prequalified candidates are treated as having already applied ([Mercor: Instant Work Offers](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/new-releases/instant-offer)). **Confirmed.**
- **The same assessment can be weighted differently** for different roles. Client needs, project scope, capacity and location or work-authorisation rules also matter ([Mercor: Assessments](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/assessments)). **Confirmed.**
- **An automated fit check** runs when you start an application. You either pass silently, see a warning (you can still apply), or are blocked for that listing only ([Mercor: Application Fit](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/announcements-and-new-releases/application-fit)). Project teams make the final decision. **Confirmed.**

## The recruitment pipeline

::: {.flow}
1. **Register**: account, profile, CV
2. **Apply**: Apply Now, complete steps, Submit
3. **Qualify**: pass the AI interview or assessment
4. **Shortlisted / prequalified**: shown to project teams
5. **Offer**: standard offer or Instant Work Offer (with a deadline)
6. **Onboarding**: ID check, background check, contract, Stripe, Insightful
7. **Paid work starts**: hours tracked, paid weekly
:::

| Stage | What it means | What it does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| Applied | Application submitted | Someone has reviewed it |
| Qualified | Your interview or assessment met the bar | A project currently needs you |
| Shortlisted / prequalified | Project teams can see you | You will get an offer |
| Offer | A contract is in your Contracts tab | Work can start immediately |
| Onboarded | All checks passed | Hours are guaranteed |
| Paid work | Hours tracked in Insightful | The project will last |

## Why qualified people still hear nothing

1. **No current demand.** Projects open and close with client demand. **Confirmed.**
2. **Assessment weighting differs by role.** **Confirmed.**
3. **Location or work-authorisation rules.** **Confirmed.**
4. **A profile that is too generic** for the matching system to place ([Remote Work Union](https://www.remoteworkunion.com/blog/mercor-no-response-after-applying)). **Supported, limited.**
5. **Unsubmitted applications.** Completed steps are not reviewed until you click **Submit** ([Mercor: Apply](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/apply)). **Confirmed.**
6. **Projects end or change.** In November 2025 the Musen project ended and contractors were offered a replacement project at a lower rate ([VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/enterprises/world-s-youngest-self-made-billionaires-face-backlash-over-alleged-pay-cuts-at-ai-startup-mercor-4965231.html)). **Supported, limited.**

## Protecting yourself from false AI flags (the evidence-based way)

The community fear is real. The fix is to make your own work easy to prove, not to play games with punctuation.

1. **Draft in a tool with version history** (Google Docs, a local file with autosave) so you can show how your answer developed.
2. **Write in your natural voice.** Don't polish every sentence into generic, AI-sounding prose. Keep your usual vocabulary and sentence length.
3. **Never paste text from an AI tool**, even for "just the intro". Mercor's [LLM usage policy](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/llm-usage) prohibits using LLMs to evaluate model responses, and its contracts prohibit LLM use on tasks.
4. **Work at a human pace.** News reports say reviewers on AI-training projects watched for work finished suspiciously fast ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/chatgpt-contractors-removed-using-ai-1875789)).
5. **If you're flagged,** respond calmly with your drafts, timestamps and reasoning through the official channel (support@mercor.com or your project lead).

# Eligibility & Account Setup {#m3}

## Who can join?

- **Payment country.** Mercor pays through **Stripe Connect** in most countries, and through **Wise** for Brazil, Nepal, Turkey and the UAE. There is also a list of unsupported countries ([Mercor: Supported Countries](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/supported-countries)). **Confirmed.**
- **Indonesia is listed** among the supported Asian countries, paid through Stripe Connect. **Confirmed** (as of 4 November 2026).
- **Payment-country eligibility is not listing eligibility.** Your country may be supported for payouts while a specific listing is restricted to, for example, US residents. Always read the location requirements on each listing.

## Creating your account

1. Go to **[work.mercor.com](https://work.mercor.com/)**. You can browse Explore before signing up.
2. Sign up with **passwordless email verification** or **Google** ([Mercor: Create Account](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/create-account)).
3. Use the **same email as your LinkedIn** if you plan to use LinkedIn Easy Apply.
4. Enter accurate details: legal first and last name, email, phone, city and country.
5. Upload your CV (Chapter 4) and add LinkedIn, or mark that you don't have one.
6. Answer **work authorisation** questions truthfully.
7. Prepare a **bank account in your own name** in a supported country. Digital wallets, prepaid cards and Wise balances are not valid payout destinations.

> **Never** use a VPN, proxy, fake address or second account. Since **17 July 2026 at 23:59 UTC**, sign-ins through VPNs, proxies and anonymising networks are **blocked** (403 error), and contracts can be paused. Running more than one Mercor account is prohibited ([Mercor: Account Security & Conduct](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/account-security-conduct)). **Confirmed.**

## The community's "domain CV" method

The most-cited community success story is about **structure, not volume**: a CV reorganised into a few clean domains got matched where a generic CV had failed (see Chapter 2, tactic 2). Here's how to apply that idea honestly:

1. **Pick 1–3 domains** you can defend in an interview (for example "Finance & spreadsheet modelling", "Indonesian language & localisation", "Python data analysis").
2. **Start each role's bullets with the domain-relevant evidence.**
3. **Name tools exactly** ("Microsoft Excel", "Python (pandas)", "Looker Studio").
4. **Use the listing's terms only where they're true.** "Evaluated documents against a written rubric" can honestly become "rubric-based quality evaluation". "AI evaluation experience" cannot, unless you've actually graded AI output.
5. **Remove noise.** Unrelated side jobs and long skill lists make matching harder, not easier.

# Using AI to Match Your CV to Jobs {#m5}

Use AI for **research and admin**: reading your CV, reading listings and scoring fit. **Never** use it to answer interviews, assessments or project tasks ([Mercor: LLM Usage Policy](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/llm-usage)).

## The 8-step workflow

::: {.flow}
1. Upload your latest CV to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini
2. Extract proven skills, experience, education and tools
3. Build your competency profile
4. Collect listings from Explore (copy text and links)
5. Compare each listing with your profile
6. Flag hard requirements and skill gaps
7. Prioritise listings where you are genuinely competitive
8. Output a table with direct application links
:::

## Reusable CV-to-Mercor matching prompt

```text
You are a recruitment analyst screening Mercor listings for my CV.
Rules: never add experience, degrees or skills that are not in my CV.
The fit score is a screening heuristic, NOT a prediction of being hired.

STEP 1: Build my competency profile from the CV:
- Experience (roles, years, domains), education, licences/certifications
- PROVEN skills and tools (quote the CV line as evidence)
- Languages and levels
- My strongest domains (max 3)

STEP 2: For EACH listing I paste:
A. Hard filters (if any FAILS → score 0, label "NOT ELIGIBLE"):
   - Allowed locations vs my country: [COUNTRY]
   - Required work authorisation
   - Mandatory degree/licence
   - Mandatory language
B. Score 0–100 (only if all hard filters pass):
   - Experience match: 0–30
   - Technical skills/tools match: 0–20
   - Education/credentials: 0–15
   - Language: 0–10
   - Availability vs listing hours: 0–10
   - Equipment (OS, device, required software): 0–10
   - Relevant Mercor assessments I've already passed: 0–5
   One sentence per component, citing CV evidence.
C. Skill gaps and honest ways to close them.
D. Advertised pay and type (hourly/per task).
E. Required assessments (if stated).
F. Recommendation: APPLY NOW / APPLY IF TIME / SKIP, with the reason.

STEP 3: Final table, highest score first:
| Title | Link | Hard filters passed? | Score | Pay | Assessment | Reason | Next action |

My country: [...]  Work authorisation: [...]  Hours per week: [...]
Device: [Windows/macOS + version]  Mercor assessments already passed: [...]

CV:
"""[PASTE CV]"""

LISTINGS (title + link + full description, one by one):
"""[PASTE LISTINGS]"""
```

The scoring model is transparent on purpose. Each component has a clear weight, and hard filters (location, work authorisation, mandatory credentials, mandatory language) cannot be offset by a high score elsewhere.

# Finding & Applying, Page by Page {#m6}

## Using Explore

Open **[work.mercor.com/explore](https://work.mercor.com/explore)**. After signing in, Explore is in the left sidebar. Search by title or keyword, then sort (the interface may change):

| Sort | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Job fit** | Prioritises listings that match your profile | First daily sweep |
| **Newest** | Recently posted roles | Every day (community tactic #5) |
| **Trending** | Popular opportunities | Spot projects that are actively hiring |
| **Most pay** | Highest pay first | Check expert roles you may have overlooked |

A Reddit recruiting post in June 2026 described **1,000+ open roles** across tech, science, healthcare, finance, translation and video evaluation ([Reddit mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1u7g7ip/snapshots/2026-06-16T23%3A15%3A44.326742Z)). **Anecdotal.** With that many listings, one page of results is never enough.

## Page-by-page screening (mandatory)

If you, or a browser agent you use, review listings:

1. Open **every** accessible page. With infinite scroll, keep scrolling until no new results load.
2. Search several relevant categories, then repeat with **synonyms and transferable skills** ("Indonesian", "Bahasa", "Southeast Asia", "bilingual", "linguist", "translator", "audio").
3. Check active and **recently added** listings (Newest).
4. Open **every** description. Record location, work-authorisation and assessment requirements **exactly**.
5. Remove duplicates and mark listings you've already applied to.
6. Log the **last page and filters** you checked in your tracker.
7. A search result does **not** prove a listing is still open. Confirm on the listing page.

> Never claim to have reviewed pages you couldn't access.

## How applying works (official)

1. Pick a listing and click **Apply Now**.
2. Complete the steps: CV, work authorisation, AI interview, forms or written assessments. Most can be done in any order. Some applications lock steps until the previous one is finished.
3. **Forms are final** once submitted.
4. Click **Submit**. Without it, project teams won't review your application.

Sources: [Mercor: Apply](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/apply), [Mercor: New Application Steps](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/new-releases/new-application-steps). **Confirmed.**

**LinkedIn Easy Apply.** Some Mercor roles accept LinkedIn Easy Apply. Your LinkedIn profile and attached CV are used for initial screening. If you pass, you get an email with a secure link to finish on work.mercor.com. Use the same email on both. **Confirmed.** We found no evidence that LinkedIn improves or hurts your odds. **Unverified.**

## How broadly should you apply?

| Strategy | Apply to | Pros | Costs & risks | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | Scores ≥80 only | Efficient, high-quality applications | Few chances; depends on few projects | Busy specialists |
| **Balanced** | Scores ≥60 | Wide but relevant | Needs a disciplined tracker | **Most applicants (recommended)** |
| **Broad** | Everything you're **genuinely eligible** for and can prove | Most chances; exploits reusable assessments | Time spent on weak matches; quality can slip | Full-time job seekers |

**What matters for volume:**

- **Applying to too few listings limits you**, because projects open and close with client demand.
- **Shared assessments make later applications cheap.** Pass once and you don't retake for comparable roles; results auto-fill ([Mercor: Assessments](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/assessments)). **Confirmed.** After a big assessment, hunt for other listings that use it. These are your highest-value, lowest-effort applications.
- **Does past activity affect future matching?** Not documented. **Unverified.**
- **When to stop chasing a listing:** it closes, the fit check blocks you, or 3–4 weeks pass with no status change after you've completed every step. Redirect effort to new listings and assessments. **Editorial recommendation.**
- **No prohibited automation.** Use AI for research, CV review and preparation only. Don't use bots to fill in applications, and never use AI to answer interviews or assessments.

# Maximising Assessment Coverage {#m7}

The single most important idea in this guide: **on Mercor, a passed assessment keeps working for you even when you're not applying.**

## How the Assessments page works

Open **[work.mercor.com/home?tab=assessments](https://work.mercor.com/home?tab=assessments)**.

**Link to Instant Work Offers.** Mercor's FAQ says a regularly updated, **assessment-complete** profile with clear availability is the biggest driver of Instant Work Offers. **Confirmed.** Completing relevant assessments **before** new listings appear can put you straight into the prequalified pool when a project opens.

> **Don't retake the same interview every day.** The limit is three, and each retake resets application progress. Save retakes for technical failures or a clearly poor performance.

## Daily proactive routine (15–30 minutes)

1. Check **new recommended** assessments.
2. Check **available** assessments that match your real skills.
3. Resume **incomplete** assessments.
4. Look for **categories you don't have yet**.
5. Check applications with **new or changed** assessment requirements.
6. Check **invitations and time-limited work trials**.

When a useful assessment appears, prepare, then complete it **yourself** in a quiet, uninterrupted block.

## Prioritising assessments

Score each criterion 1–5 and add them up (maximum 35):

| Criterion | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance to your profession | Far from your expertise | Core expertise |
| Number of roles likely to use it | One niche listing | Many listings on Explore |
| Reuse across projects | No | Clearly yes |
| Your likelihood of doing well | Low | High |
| Time required | Very long | Short |
| Recommended by Mercor | No | Yes (in Recommended) |
| Adds a new competency signal | Duplicates an old one | New domain or data type |

**Rule of thumb:** do ≥25 first, then 18–24. Skip <18 unless you have lots of spare time.

| CV-based questions | The AI probes your CV, including one-line mentions; for example, listing Node.js led to questions on event-driven architecture ([Glassdoor](https://fr.glassdoor.ca/Entretien/Mercor-Entretien-E9031572-RVW100314407.htm)) | Supported, limited |

| Confidentiality | Don't share specific questions or screenshots ([PracHub](https://prachub.com/resources/mercor-ai-interview-guide-2026-20-minute-voice-screen-retakes-screen-sharing-and-score-reuse)) | Supported, limited |
| Technical problems | Email **support@mercor.com** with a screenshot or recording and the interview name | Confirmed |

## Community interview habits worth copying

- **Know every line of your CV.** Prepare a one- or two-sentence explanation for each item. This is the most consistent advice across Glassdoor, Exponent and blog write-ups. **Supported, limited.**
- **Go one layer deeper.** The AI asks follow-ups on what you just said. Have one detailed story per major role. **Supported, limited.**
- **Keep answers short and structured.** A long silence can make the AI cut in. **Supported, limited.**
- **Say "I", not just "we".** Make your personal contribution clear. **Unverified** as a scoring factor, but good practice.
- **Use headphones with a decent mic** and a stable connection. Several candidates report audio problems. **Supported, limited.**

## Practice drills by role

All questions below are **original simulations** by Next Offer Academy, not real Mercor questions.

| Role | Practice question | What a strong answer shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Generalist** | "Two AI answers to the same prompt: one is longer and friendlier but cites a wrong date; the other is short and correct. Which is better and why?" | Accuracy and instruction-following first; specific reasoning |
| **Domain expert** | "Walk me through a complex case you handled. What was the key decision and what evidence supported it?" | Depth, precise terminology, professional judgement |
| **Business & consulting** | "How would you size the cold-chain logistics market in Indonesia?" | Structured assumptions, data sources, sensitivity |
| **Finance & spreadsheets** | "Your model's cash balance goes negative in year 3. How do you investigate?" | Systematic checks (formulas, assumptions, circularity), clear communication |
| **Programming** | "This function passes the tests but is O(n²). Is it correct? What would you change?" | Correctness vs efficiency; edge cases |
| **Science & technical** | "A model explains a chemistry concept fluently but confuses two reaction types. How do you score it?" | Catching subtle errors; scoring on accuracy |
| **Indonesian language** | "Explain the difference between *diundur* and *dimajukan* and how an AI might mistranslate them." | Nuance, contextual examples, register |

**Self-review rubric (1–5) for your recordings:**

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevance | Doesn't answer | Partly answers | Hits the core of the question |
| Evidence | No example | Generic example | Specific example with numbers or outcomes |
| Reasoning | Jumps to conclusions | Some logic, incomplete | Clear assumptions, trade-offs, conclusion |
| Expertise | Wrong terms | Correct but shallow | Accurate and deep |
| Communication | Rambling, long pauses | Clear enough | Concise, structured, confident |

**How to practise:**

1. Write a one- or two-sentence explanation for **every CV line**.
2. Prepare 5 project stories (problem → your action → result).
3. Record yourself answering 5 practice questions and score them with the rubric.
4. Practise with an AI simulator **before** the official interview (never during it).
5. For roles with screen sharing, practise explaining while showing a file, code or spreadsheet.

# Indonesia-Specific Guidance {#m10}

| Topic | What to know |
|---|---|
| **Can Indonesians join?** | Yes. Indonesia is listed as supported, with payouts through Stripe Connect ([Mercor](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/supported-countries)). **Confirmed** |
| **Payments** | To an Indonesian bank account in your own name, in USD converted by the payment provider. Stripe holds the first payout for 7 days, then weekly. E-wallets are not supported |
| **Indonesia-specific listings** | Indonesian and bilingual Indonesian–English listings exist (Chapter 5), with advertised pay of roughly US$10–50/h |
| **International listings** | Many generalist and domain-expert listings are open globally, but **some are restricted** (e.g. US only). Read each listing's location rules |
| **English** | Interviews are usually in English. Explaining your experience clearly matters a lot |
| **Time zone** | Hourly projects are usually flexible, but meetings and Slack activity may follow US hours. State WIB/UTC+7 and your overlap hours |
| **Contractor paperwork** | Non-US contractors complete a **W-8BEN** in Stripe and don't receive a 1099 |
| **Tax** | Mercor income must still be reported in your Indonesian annual tax return (SPT Tahunan). Confirm the calculation with a tax adviser or your local tax office (KPP) |
| **Equipment** | Windows 10+ or macOS 11+; avoid locked-down office laptops; keep a hotspot as backup |
| **Community signal** | We found no systematic discussion of Indonesian applicants' outcomes; treat global anecdotes with care |

**Competing internationally without pretending to be in the US:**

1. **Target listings open to Indonesia, Southeast Asia or worldwide.** Claiming US residency or work authorisation is a serious violation, and VPNs are blocked.
2. **Lean into language.** Native Indonesian (and regional languages such as Javanese or Sundanese) is a rare skill globally.
3. **Build assessment coverage.** Passed generalist and language assessments keep you in the running for future projects automatically.
4. **Show domain depth** (Indonesian law, healthcare, finance, engineering) with concrete evidence.
5. **Be visible in Slack once onboarded,** even if that means checking in during your evening for US-hours announcements.

## If an offer is withdrawn

1. Check your email and Contracts tab for the reason (background check, scope change).
2. Email **support@mercor.com** with the project name and offer ID.
3. Return to your routine: update your profile and continue assessments. Other offers can still arrive through matching.

## Avoiding scams

- Mercor **never** charges to apply, interview, train or "guarantee placement".
- Official email comes from **@mercor.com**; active contractors also get **@mercor.expert** addresses ([Remowork](https://remowork.life/blog/mercor-review-how-it-works-pay-and-is-it-worth-it-in-2026)). **Supported, limited.**
- Payment by PayPal or crypto is a red flag ([GPTPrompts](https://gptprompts.ai/mercor-guide)).
- Be wary of "recruiters" on LinkedIn, X or Telegram promising Mercor jobs, and of anyone asking for OTPs, passwords or ID photos over chat. Referral links are legitimate, but a referral link never guarantees anything.
- Official help: **support@mercor.com** (technical/project), **work@mercor.com** (US work authorisation), and [talent.docs.mercor.com](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/welcome).

3. **Scanned CVs.** Image PDFs can't be parsed.
4. **A generic profile** that lists everything and proves nothing.
5. **Burning interview retakes as practice.** Each one resets application progress.
6. **Using ChatGPT or Claude during interviews, assessments or tasks.** It breaks policy and gets people removed.
7. **VPNs or fake locations.** Blocked since July 2026 and can pause contracts.
8. **Applying to listings you're not eligible for** (e.g. US-only).
9. **Missing Instant Offer deadlines**, or accepting the first work trial when a better one is pending.
10. **Treating an offer as income.** Onboarding, checks, pauses and cancellations are normal.
11. **Ignoring Slack after onboarding** and missing task drops.
12. **Poor time tracking.** Untracked hours aren't paid.
13. **Relying only on Mercor.** Projects end; keep other platforms warm.
14. **Paying for "guaranteed placement"** or trusting random referral DMs.

**Risky, misleading or prohibited:** multiple accounts, VPNs/proxies, false location or work authorisation, invented experience, using AI to answer assessments or evaluate model outputs, logging hours while not working, self-referrals, and tampering with monitoring tools.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Start Interview button greyed out | Allow camera and mic in browser settings; share your **entire screen** if required |
| 403 error on sign-in | Turn off VPNs, proxies or other IP-masking features; use a normal home or mobile connection |
| CV parsed badly | Re-export as a text-based PDF ≤3 MB from Google Docs or Word |
| Interview dropped | Email support@mercor.com with a screenshot or recording and the interview name |
| Background check over 7 business days | Contact the provider and Mercor support with your report ID |
| Island/Okta signs into the wrong account | Click Back, then sign in with your personal email after the @mercor.expert step |
| Hours not tracking | Make sure Insightful is running and online before you start |
| No news after applying | Keep the routine: refine the profile, take another assessment, apply to new listings, and apply on other platforms |
| Flagged for possible AI use | Respond calmly with drafts, version history and reasoning via official channels |
