## How this was researched, and its limits

- **Method:** web search of public TikTok topic pages, news and trade coverage of #WorkTok/#CareerTok, music-licensing guides, and job boards.
- **What could not be done from this environment:** TikTok, Instagram, Threads and X pages could not be opened directly (DNS/network block), and TikTok Creative Center was not reachable. So **per-video engagement numbers, exact publish dates, and sound names were NOT available** and are marked "not available". Nothing below is estimated or invented.
- **To fill the gaps (5 minutes, owner):** open TikTok Creative Center → Trends → Hashtags/Songs, set Region = United States and Industry = "Education" or "Business Services", and paste the top 5 results into `trend_references.csv`. Do the same weekly; the weekly review uses it.

## Music and audio: the rule that changes everything

- **TikTok business accounts** can only use the **Commercial Music Library (CML)**; most trending songs are not in it ([Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/commercially-licensed-vs-trending-sounds-what-can-brands-use), [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-library-explained)). CML licenses cover TikTok only.
- **Instagram business/professional accounts** are limited to the **Meta Sound Collection** and a reduced library; availability in the picker isn't the same as commercial permission ([Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/instagram-music-library), [Foxi Music guide](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/instagram-reels-music-copyright-legal-guide/)).
- **What we do:** each post's audio suggestion names a **mood/tempo** and where to pick it (TikTok: Add sound → Commercial sounds → Trending; Instagram: Audio → For You / Meta Sound Collection), or recommends **original voiceover** (always allowed). We do **not** name specific "trending songs", because we can't verify trend status or licensing from here.
- Verify these rules in TikTok's Business Help Center and Meta's music help pages; vendor blogs are the sources above.

## Recurring formats that work in this niche (patterns → our adaptation)

| Pattern | What top posts do | Our original version |
|---|---|---|
| **Job drop listicle** | 3–5 named employers, pay on screen, 2–3 s each | "Jobs we actually checked this week": only verified listings, with the date checked |
| **Green screen over a posting** | Creator points at a listing screenshot | Owner films over our **job card** asset (our own design summarizing the listing; no copied UI) |
| **Myth check / "is this legit?"** | Reacting to a claim | "We fact-checked 3 viral remote job posts", our signature series |
| **3 fixes in 30 seconds** | Resume/LinkedIn mistakes, before → after | Same, with our original examples |
| **Receipts / real numbers** | Earnings screenshots, application tallies | Only **documented** numbers from cited sources (never invented) |
| **Opinion + question (Threads/X)** | Short take, ends with a question | Personal-style posts from the owner's real perspective |
| **Notes-app / text-on-screen** | Lo-fi, phone-native look | Our "notes" and "caption bubble" templates |
