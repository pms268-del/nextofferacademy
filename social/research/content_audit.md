# Content Audit: Week 1 (Oct 27 – Nov 2) Instagram & TikTok

*Audited Oct 27, 2026 against the trend research in `trend_reference_library.md`.*

## Verdict

The facts were solid (every number sourced), but the **packaging read as a brand slideshow**: same dark gradient on every slide, title-card hooks, no faces or native-looking elements, no music plan, and videos that were basically animated carousels. That is why it felt generic and AI-made.

| Problem | Where | Fix in week 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Hooks were headlines, not curiosity ("5 AI training gigs open to US residents") | All 14 | First-3-second hooks built on tension: myth check, warning, specific number |
| Every slide/scene looked identical (navy gradient, brand bar) | All media | New lo-fi templates: caption bubbles, notes-app, job cards. Brand kept small |
| No people, no native features | All videos | Variation B in every topic: a film-yourself script (green screen / talking head) |
| Silent videos with no audio plan | 7 TikToks | Audio suggestion per post (CML/Meta-safe), Notify-Me mode for in-app music |
| Too long and slow (30–34 s, 4–6 s per scene) | 7 TikToks | 15–25 s, 2–3.5 s per scene |
| Same post format daily (carousel) | 7 IG | Mix of Reels (4) and carousels (3) |
| Captions sounded like a press release | Most | Shorter, first-person-plural, one question at the end |
| No real application links | Most | Exact listing URLs in caption (where IG allows) and in the bio-link notes |
| Generic promotion day | Nov 2 IG | Promo folded into useful content as a soft CTA |

## Keep (useful assets)

- Day 2 "Spot the mistake" TikTok concept (interactive), now reworked.
- Day 5 W-2 vs gig angle, and the scam warning, moved into the "real vs fake job post" topic.
- All fact sources, the opportunity database, and the fact-check gate.

The 14 week-1 Buffer drafts were **replaced in place** by the improved week-2 posts (same Buffer post IDs, new media, captions, dates and Notify-Me mode). Nothing was published. Old files stay in `social/content/2026-10-27 … 2026-11-02` for reference.
