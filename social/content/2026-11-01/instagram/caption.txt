AI can tailor your resume in minutes, but only if you give it rules.

The 45-minute workflow:
1️⃣ Decode the job: must-haves + exact keywords
2️⃣ Fit check: score 1–10, skip below 6
3️⃣ Tailor with rules: "Use only my facts. Never invent numbers."
4️⃣ Test the PDF: copy-paste it into a text editor

Golden rule: if you can't explain a line in an interview, delete it.
