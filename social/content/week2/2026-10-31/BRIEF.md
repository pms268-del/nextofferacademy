## Variation B: film it yourself

**Hook:** I'm not saying every 'no experience' remote job post is a scam. But this one is.

**Format:** Green screen over the 'real vs fake' card (no real scammer screenshots or usernames)

**Script:**

1. Not every 'no experience' remote job post is a scam. But here's how to spot the ones that are.
2. Red flag one: the caption has a phone number and says DM me. Real jobs live on the company's careers page.
3. Red flag two: you have to pay for anything. Training, equipment, a deposit. No.
4. Red flag three: they move you to WhatsApp or Telegram.
5. The FTC got about twenty thousand task-scam reports in the first half of 2024. Report them at ReportFraud dot FTC dot gov.

**Shooting notes:** Use the carousel slides as green-screen backgrounds. Never show a real person's username or number.
