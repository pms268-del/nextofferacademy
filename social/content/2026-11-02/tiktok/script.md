**Full voiceover script:**

Remote job scams are exploding. Here's how to spot one in ten seconds. The FTC says task scam reports went from about five thousand in all of 2023 to about twenty thousand in just the first half of 2024. Job scam losses topped two hundred twenty million dollars in those six months. Red flag one: you have to pay. For training, equipment, or to unlock earnings. Red flag two: a recruiter texts you out of nowhere and moves you to WhatsApp or Telegram. Red flag three: a chat-only interview with an instant offer. Remember: legit jobs pay you. You never pay them. Report scams at report fraud dot f-t-c dot gov. Follow for safe, verified jobs.
