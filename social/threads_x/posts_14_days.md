## Day 5

**Threads** · finding · 305 chars

> The FTC got about 5,000 task-scam reports in all of 2023.
> 
> In the first half of 2024 alone: about 20,000.
> 
> The pattern: a "recruiter" texts you, moves you to WhatsApp, has you do simple tasks, then asks you to pay to unlock your earnings.
> 
> Real jobs never ask you to pay. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Source: social/research/week2_fact_sources.md

## Day 8

**Threads** · opinion · owner voice · 333 chars

> "Ghost jobs" are listings that stay up but aren't really being filled, and every estimate of how common they are is different.
> 
> What you can check yourself: is it on the company's own careers page, does it have a recent date, has it been reposted unchanged for months?
> 
> If it fails all three, don't spend an hour on the cover letter.
